Aglaonema

Araceae


Light

| Low

Bright, indirect light. Direct sunlight can cause leaf burn, while too little light can cause poor growth and dull foilage.

Water

| Low

Keep the soil moist, but not waterlogged. Water when the top inch of soil feels dry. Aglaonema are sensitive to overwatering and can develop root rot if left in soggy soil.

Soil

| Well-Drained

Use a well-draining potting mix rich in organic matter. A mix of peat, perlite, and pine bark works well to provide the necessary drainage and nutrients.

Humidity

| High

Thrives in warm, humid environments. Ideal temperatures range from 65°F to 80°F (18°C to 27°C). Maintain high humidity levels, ideally above 60%, by misting the leaves regularly or using a humidifier.


Aglaonema is a stunning genus of tropical and subtropical plants, celebrated for its vibrant, patterned foliage. With around 21 species, each showcases unique leaf shapes, colors, and textures from deep greens to reds. Known for being resilient and low-maintenance, Aglaonemas thrive both indoors and in gardens.


Fertilization

Feed Aglaonema plants with a balanced, water-soluble fertilizer every 2-4 weeks during the growing season (spring and summer). Reduce feeding in the fall and winter when the plant's growth slows down.

Maintenance

Remove any yellow or damaged leaves to maintain the plant's appearance and health. Repot Aglaonema plants every 1-2 years to refresh the soil and accommodate growth.

Physical Characteristics

Aglaonema plants are celebrated for their eye-catching, decorative foliage. Their leaves are generally large, ranging from oval to lance-shaped, and can feature colors from rich green to striking patterns of silver, pink, red, or white. Prominent, contrasting veins enhance their dramatic look, while short, mostly hidden stems give the plant a full, bushy appearance.

Common Names

Evergreen, Philippine Evergreen, Aglaonema

Common Species

1. Aglaonema 'Silver Bay':
- Features large, silver-green leaves with dark green edges.
- Ideal for indoor growing, prefers bright, indirect light and high humidity.

2. Aglaonema 'Red Siam':
- Known for its vibrant red and green leaves.
- Thrives in bright, indirect light and warm, humid conditions.

3. Aglaonema 'Maria':
- Notable for its dark green leaves with light green markings.
- Requires bright, indirect light and well-draining soil.

4. Aglaonema 'Emerald Bay':
- Dramatic leaves with a mix of silver and green shades.
- Prefers full shade and high humidity.

5. Aglaonema 'Cory':
- Features green leaves with a splash of cream or yellow.
- Grows best in bright, indirect light and high humidity.

6. Aglaonema 'Cutlass':
- Has long, narrow leaves with silver and green variegation.
- Ideal for indoor conditions, thrives in indirect light and high humidity.

7. Aglaonema 'Tigress':
- Leaves with a unique pattern resembling tiger stripes.
- Often associated with good fortune and used in interior decor.

8. Aglaonema 'Golden Madonna':
- Thick, velvety leaves with a mix of green and yellow hues.
- Prefers high humidity and protection from direct sunlight.

9. Aglaonema 'Crete':
- Brightly colored leaves with pink, green, and red hues.
- Grows well in partial sunlight and requires high humidity.

10. Aglaonema 'Silver Queen':
- Long, lance-shaped leaves with silvery variegation, known for its easy care.
- Requires high humidity and bright, indirect light.

11. Aglaonema 'Emerald Beauty':
- Leaves with dark green edges and a silvery center.
- Prefers high humidity and bright, indirect light.

12. Aglaonema 'White Rain':
- Deep green leaves with white streaks.
- Grows well in typical Aglaonema conditions with indirect light and high humidity.
