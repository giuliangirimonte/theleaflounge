Begonia

Begoniaceae


Light

| Low

Aralia plants prefer bright, indirect light. Direct sunlight can scorch their leaves, while too little light can lead to poor growth and dull foliage.

Water

| Medium/High

Keep the soil consistently moist but not waterlogged. Water when the top inch of soil feels dry. Aralia plants are sensitive to overwatering and can develop root rot if left in soggy soil.

Soil

| Well-Drained

Use a well-draining potting mix rich in organic matter. A mix of peat, perlite, and pine bark works well to provide the necessary drainage and nutrients.

Humidity

| High

Aralia plants thrive in moderate to warm environments. Ideal temperatures range from 60°F to 75°F (15°C to 24°C). Maintain high humidity levels, ideally above 50%, by misting the leaves regularly or using a humidifier.


Begonia, commonly referred to as Wax Begonia or Rex Begonia, is a captivating genus of tropical and subtropical plants renowned for their striking foliage and colorful flowers. The genus includes over 1,800 species, each boasting unique leaf shapes, sizes, and colors. Begonia plants are prized for their ornamental value and are popular both as houseplants and garden specimens.


Fertilization

Feed Aralia plants with a balanced, water-soluble fertilizer every 4-6 weeks during the growing season (spring and summer). Reduce feeding in the fall and winter when the plant's growth slows down.

Maintenance

Remove any yellow or damaged leaves to maintain the plant's appearance and health. Repot Aralia plants every 1-2 years to refresh the soil and accommodate growth.

Physical Characteristics

Begonia plants are known for their striking, ornamental foliage and colorful flowers. The leaves are typically asymmetrical, often with intricate patterns and vibrant colors ranging from green to deep red, purple, and silver. The flowers can be single or double and come in a variety of colors, including pink, red, white, and yellow. The stems can be succulent, fibrous, or tuberous, depending on the species.

Common Names

Wax Begonia, Rex Begonia, Angel Wing Begonia, Tuberous Begonia, Rhizomatous Begonia

Order: Cucurbitales | Family: Begoniaceae | Genus: Begonia


Common Species

1. Begonia rex (Rex Begonia):
- Features large, colorful leaves with intricate patterns.
- Ideal for indoor growing, prefers bright, indirect light and high humidity.

2. Begonia maculata (Polka Dot Begonia):
- Known for its polka-dotted leaves and white flowers.
- Thrives in bright, indirect light and well-draining soil.

3. Begonia semperflorens (Wax Begonia):
- Notable for its waxy leaves and continuous blooming.
- Requires bright, indirect light and can tolerate lower humidity.

4. Begonia boliviensis (Bolivian Begonia):
- Dramatic, pendulous flowers in shades of red, orange, and pink.
- Prefers full shade to partial sunlight and high humidity.

5. Begonia cucullata (Clubed Begonia):
- Features small, heart-shaped leaves and white to pink flowers.
- Grows well in bright, indirect light and moderate humidity.

6. Begonia 'Escargot':
- Has unique, snail-like leaf patterns in shades of green and silver.
- Ideal for indoor conditions, thrives in indirect light and high humidity.

7. Begonia grandis (Hardy Begonia):
- Small plant with pink or white flowers and heart-shaped leaves.
- Often associated with good fortune and used in Buddhist temples.

8. Begonia venosa:
- Thick, velvety leaves that are jade green to bluish-green.
- Prefers high humidity and protection from direct sunlight.

9. Begonia pavonina (Peacock Begonia):
- Iridescent blue-green leaves with a metallic sheen.
- Grows well in partial sunlight and requires high humidity.

10. Begonia masoniana (Iron Cross Begonia):
- Heart-shaped leaves with a dark, cross-shaped pattern.
- Requires high humidity and bright, indirect light.

11. Begonia erythrophylla (Beefsteak Begonia):
- Rounded, glossy leaves with a deep red underside.
- Prefers high humidity and bright, indirect light.

12. Begonia luxurians (Palm Leaf Begonia):
- Palm-like foliage with elongated, finger-like leaflets.
- Grows well in typical Begonia conditions with indirect light and high humidity.
