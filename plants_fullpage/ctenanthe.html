Ctenanthe

Marantaceae


Light

| Medium/High

Ctenanthe plants prefer bright, indirect light. Direct sunlight can scorch their leaves, while too little light can lead to poor growth and dull foliage.

Water

| Medium/High

Keep the soil consistently moist but not waterlogged. Water when the top inch of soil feels dry. Ctenanthe plants are sensitive to overwatering and can develop root rot if left in soggy soil.

Soil

| Well-Drained

Use a well-draining potting mix rich in organic matter. A mix of peat, perlite, and pine bark works well to provide the necessary drainage and nutrients.

Humidity

| High

Ctenanthe plants thrive in warm, humid environments. Ideal temperatures range from 65°F to 80°F (18°C to 27°C). Maintain high humidity levels, ideally above 60%, by misting the leaves regularly or using a humidifier.


Ctenanthe, commonly referred to as Never Never Plant or Brazilian Snow Plant, is a captivating genus of tropical plants renowned for their striking foliage. The genus includes around 15 species, each boasting unique leaf shapes, sizes, and colors. Ctenanthe plants are prized for their ornamental value and are popular both as houseplants and garden specimens.


Fertilization

Feed Ctenanthe plants with a balanced, water-soluble fertilizer every 2-4 weeks during the growing season (spring and summer). Reduce feeding in the fall and winter when the plant's growth slows down.

Maintenance

Remove any yellow or damaged leaves to maintain the plant's appearance and health. Repot Ctenanthe plants every 1-2 years to refresh the soil and accommodate growth.

Physical Characteristics

Ctenanthe plants are known for their striking, ornamental foliage. The leaves are typically large, elongated, and can vary in color from deep green to silver, with various patterns and markings. The leaf veins are often prominent and contrasting, adding to the plant's dramatic appearance. The stems can be short and hidden by the leaves, or tall and upright, depending on the species.

Common Names

Never Never Plant, Brazilian Snow Plant, Prayer Plant (for some species)

Order: Zingiberales | Family: Marantaceae | Genus: Ctenanthe


Common Species

1. Ctenanthe burle-marxii (Fishbone Prayer Plant):
- Features elongated, green leaves with striking silver stripes resembling fishbones.
- Ideal for indoor growing, prefers bright, indirect light and high humidity.

2. Ctenanthe oppenheimiana (Never Never Plant):
- Known for its large, variegated leaves with shades of green, cream, and pink.
- Thrives in bright, indirect light and warm, humid conditions.

3. Ctenanthe setosa (Grey Star):
- Notable for its elongated, silver-grey leaves with dark green margins.
- Requires bright, indirect light and well-draining soil.

4. Ctenanthe lubbersiana (Brazilian Snow Plant):
- Dramatic leaves with green and yellow variegation.
- Prefers full shade and high humidity.

5. Ctenanthe amabilis:
- Features spectacular silver leaves with dark green veins.
- Grows best in bright, indirect light and high humidity.
