Schefflera

Araliaceae


Light

| Medium/High

Schefflera plants prefer bright, indirect light. While they can tolerate lower light conditions, their growth may be slower, and the foliage less vibrant.

Water

| Medium/High

Allow the top inch of soil to dry out between waterings. Overwatering can lead to root rot, so ensure the soil is well-draining and not soggy.

Soil

| Well-Drained

Use a well-draining potting mix. A mix of peat, perlite, and standard potting soil works well to provide the necessary drainage and nutrients.

Humidity

| Medium/High

Schefflera plants thrive in warm, humid environments. Ideal temperatures range from 60°F to 75°F (16°C to 24°C). Maintain moderate to high humidity levels, ideally above 50%, by misting the leaves regularly or using a humidifier.


Schefflera, commonly known as Umbrella Plant, is a popular genus of tropical plants celebrated for their lush, attractive foliage. The genus includes several species, each offering unique leaf shapes and growth habits. Schefflera plants are favored for their adaptability and ornamental appeal, making them a staple in both indoor and outdoor settings.


Fertilization

Feed Schefflera plants with a balanced, water-soluble fertilizer every 2-4 weeks during the growing season (spring and summer). Reduce feeding in the fall and winter when the plant's growth slows down.

Maintenance

Prune Schefflera plants to maintain their shape and encourage bushier growth. Remove any yellow or damaged leaves to keep the plant healthy. Repot Schefflera plants every 1-2 years to refresh the soil and accommodate growth.

Physical Characteristics

Schefflera plants are known for their distinctive, umbrella-like foliage. The leaves are typically compound, with several glossy, oval leaflets radiating from a central point. The foliage can range in color from deep green to variegated patterns. The plant's growth habit is usually upright and can become quite large in ideal conditions.

Common Names

Umbrella Plant, Octopus Tree, Dwarf Umbrella Tree

Order: Apiales | Family: Araliaceae | Genus: Schefflera


Common Species

1. Schefflera actinophylla (Umbrella Tree):
- Features large, glossy, umbrella-like leaf clusters.
- Thrives in bright, indirect light and warm, humid conditions.

2. Schefflera arboricola (Dwarf Umbrella Tree):
- Known for its smaller size and variegated leaves.
- Ideal for indoor growing, prefers bright, indirect light and moderate humidity.

3. Schefflera elegantissima (False Aralia):
- Notable for its finely divided, serrated leaves that are bronze-green.
- Requires bright, indirect light and well-draining soil.

4. Schefflera amate (Amate Schefflera):
- Features large, leathery leaves with a glossy surface.
- Prefers indirect light and high humidity.

5. Schefflera taiwaniana (Taiwan Schefflera):
- Known for its elegant, slender leaves that create a delicate look.
- Grows best in partial shade and high humidity.
