Calathea

Marantaceae


Light

| Medium/High

Calathea plants prefer bright, indirect light. Direct sunlight can scorch their leaves, while too little light can lead to poor growth and dull foliage.

Water

| Low

Keep the soil consistently moist but not waterlogged. Water when the top inch of soil feels dry. Calathea plants are sensitive to overwatering and can develop root rot if left in soggy soil.

Soil

| Well-Drained

Use a well-draining potting mix rich in organic matter. A mix of peat, perlite, and pine bark works well to provide the necessary drainage and nutrients.

Humidity

| High

Calathea plants thrive in warm, humid environments. Ideal temperatures range from 65°F to 80°F (18°C to 27°C). Maintain high humidity levels, ideally above 60%, by misting the leaves regularly or using a humidifier.


Calathea, commonly referred to as Peacock Plant or Prayer Plant, is a captivating genus of tropical and subtropical plants renowned for their striking foliage. The genus includes around 60 species, each boasting unique leaf patterns, sizes, and colors. Calathea plants are prized for their ornamental value and are popular both as houseplants and garden specimens.


Fertilization

Feed Calathea plants with a balanced, water-soluble fertilizer every 2-4 weeks during the growing season (spring and summer). Reduce feeding in the fall and winter when the plant's growth slows down.

Maintenance

Remove any yellow or damaged leaves to maintain the plant's appearance and health. Repot Calathea plants every 1-2 years to refresh the soil and accommodate growth.

Physical Characteristics

Calathea plants are known for their striking, ornamental foliage. The leaves are typically large, oval to lance-shaped, and can vary in color from deep green to patterned with pink, white, or purple. The leaf veins are often prominent and contrasting, adding to the plant's dramatic appearance. The stems can be short and hidden by the leaves, or tall and upright, depending on the species.

Common Names

Peacock Plant, Prayer Plant, Zebra Plant, Rattlesnake Plant, Pinstripe Plant

Order: Zingiberales | Family: Marantaceae | Genus: Calathea


Common Species

1. Calathea orbifolia:
- Features large, round leaves with silvery-green stripes.
- Ideal for indoor growing, prefers bright, indirect light and high humidity.

2. Calathea lancifolia (Rattlesnake Plant):
- Known for its long, narrow leaves with dark green spots and purple undersides.
- Thrives in bright, indirect light and warm, humid conditions.

3. Calathea roseopicta 'Medallion':
- Notable for its dark green leaves with pinkish-white markings.
- Requires bright, indirect light and well-draining soil.

4. Calathea makoyana (Peacock Plant):
- Dramatic leaves with a light green background and dark green feathering.
- Prefers full shade and high humidity.

5. Calathea rufibarba (Furry Feather):
- Features wavy-edged leaves with a velvety texture and maroon undersides.
- Grows best in bright, indirect light and high humidity.

6. Calathea crocata (Eternal Flame):
- Has dark green leaves with bright orange flowers.
- Ideal for indoor conditions, thrives in indirect light and high humidity.

7. Calathea zebrina (Zebra Plant):
- Large, lance-shaped leaves with zebra-like stripes.
- Often associated with good fortune and used in interior decor.

8. Calathea warscewiczii:
- Thick, velvety leaves with a dark green top and burgundy underside.
- Prefers high humidity and protection from direct sunlight.

9. Calathea musaica (Network Plant):
- Intricately patterned leaves that resemble a network.
- Grows well in partial sunlight and requires high humidity.

10. Calathea loeseneri:
- Long, lance-shaped leaves with a silvery sheen, known for its precise growing needs.
- Requires high humidity and bright, indirect light.

11. Calathea lietzei 'White Fusion':
- Leaves with white, green, and purple variegation.
- Prefers high humidity and bright, indirect light.

12. Calathea ornata (Pinstripe Plant):
- Deep green leaves with delicate pink pinstripes.
- Grows well in typical Calathea conditions with indirect light and high humidity.
