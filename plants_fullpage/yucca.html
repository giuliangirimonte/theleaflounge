Yucca

Asparagaceae


Light

| Medium/High

Yucca prefers bright, indirect light** to full sunlight. Place near a south or west-facing window.

Water

| Medium/High

Yucca are highly drought-tolerant and prefers dry conditions. Water only when the top 2-3 inches of soil are dry. Avoid overwatering, which can cause root rot.

Soil

| Well-Drained

Yucca thrive in well-draining, sandy or gritty soil. Cactus or succulent potting mix, or a mix of potting soil, sand, and perlite. Slightly acidic to neutral (pH 6.0 - 7.0).

Humidity

| Medium

Prefers temperatures between 60°F and 80°F (15°C to 27°C). Can tolerate cooler temperatures down to 30°F (-1°C) for short periods, depending on the variety. Low to moderate humidity does well in typical household conditions.


Yucca is a hardy, drought-tolerant plant known for its striking, sword-like leaves and dramatic architectural form. Native to the arid regions of North and Central America, Yucca is popular as both an outdoor landscaping plant and an indoor houseplant due to its resilience and low-maintenance nature.


Fertilization

Yucca do not require heavy feeding. Fertilize once every 2-3 months during the growing season (spring and summer). Balanced, water-soluble fertilizer diluted to half strength.

Maintenance

Remove dead or damaged leaves as needed. Repot every 2-3 years or when the plant becomes root-bound. Wipe leaves occasionally to remove dust and allow for better photosynthesis.

Physical Characteristics

Yucca plants typically have stiff, sword-like leaves that grow in rosettes. The leaves are often green, but some varieties have variegated or bluish hues. Mature plants produce tall, spiky flower stalks adorned with clusters of white or cream-colored bell-shaped flowers.

Common Names

Yucca, Adam’s Needle, Spanish Bayonet, Bear Grass, Soapweed

Order: Asparagales | Family: Asparagaceae | Genus: Yucca


Common Species

1. Yucca elephantipes (Spineless Yucca):
- Popular as a houseplant; softer leaves and a tree-like appearance.

2. Yucca filamentosa (Adam’s Needle):
- Common in gardens; known for its white, thread-like filaments along the leaves.

3. Yucca gloriosa (Spanish Dagger):
- Features sharp leaves and striking flower spikes.

4. Yucca rostrata (Beaked Yucca):
- Attractive bluish foliage and striking trunk structure.

5. Yucca aloifolia (Spanish Bayonet): - Known for its sharply pointed leaves and architectural beauty.
