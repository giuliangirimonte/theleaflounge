Aeschynanthus

Gesneriaceae


Light

| Medium/High

Lipstick plants prefer bright, indirect light. Direct sunlight can scorch their leaves, while too little light can lead to poor growth and reduced flowering.

Water

| Medium/High

Keep the soil consistently moist but not waterlogged. Water when the top inch of soil feels dry. Lipstick plants are sensitive to overwatering and can develop root rot if left in soggy soil.

Soil

| Well-Drained

Use a well-draining potting mix rich in organic matter. A mix of peat, perlite, and pine bark works well to provide the necessary drainage and nutrients.

Humidity

| High

Lipstick plants thrive in warm, humid environments. Ideal temperatures range from 65°F to 80°F (18°C to 27°C). Maintain high humidity levels, ideally above 60%, by misting the leaves regularly or using a humidifier.


Lipstick Plant, scientifically known as Aeschynanthus, is a captivating genus of tropical and subtropical plants renowned for their striking, tubular flowers. The genus includes around 185 species, each boasting unique flower shapes, sizes, and colors. Lipstick plants are prized for their ornamental value and are popular both as houseplants and garden specimens.


Fertilization

Feed lipstick plants with a balanced, water-soluble fertilizer every 2-4 weeks during the growing season (spring and summer). Reduce feeding in the fall and winter when the plant's growth slows down.

Maintenance

Remove any yellow or damaged leaves to maintain the plant's appearance and health. Repot lipstick plants every 1-2 years to refresh the soil and accommodate growth.

Physical Characteristics

Lipstick plants are known for their striking, tubular flowers that resemble lipstick tubes, hence the name. The flowers can vary in color from deep red to bright orange and occasionally yellow. The leaves are typically glossy, dark green, and can vary in shape from narrow and elongated to broad and rounded. The stems are often trailing or arching, making them ideal for hanging baskets.

Common Names

Lipstick Plant, Basket Vine, Lipstick Vine

Order: Lamiales | Family: Gesneriaceae | Genus: Aeschynanthus


Common Species

1. Aeschynanthus radicans (Lipstick Plant):
- Features bright red, tubular flowers with dark green, glossy leaves.
- Ideal for indoor growing, prefers bright, indirect light and high humidity.

2. Aeschynanthus longicaulis (Zebra Basket Vine):
- Known for its unique, mottled leaves and tubular red flowers.
- Thrives in bright, indirect light and warm, humid conditions.

3. Aeschynanthus lobbianus:
- Notable for its arching stems and bright red flowers with a yellow throat.
- Requires bright, indirect light and well-draining soil.

4. Aeschynanthus japhrolepis (Twisted Lipstick Plant):
- Dramatic twisted stems with orange-red flowers.
- Prefers full shade and high humidity.

5. Aeschynanthus marmoratus (Mottled Lipstick Plant):
- Features spectacularly mottled leaves with deep green veins resembling scales.
- Grows best in bright, indirect light and high humidity.

6. Aeschynanthus speciosus:
- Has thick, dark green leaves with bright orange-red flowers.
- Ideal for indoor conditions, thrives in indirect light and high humidity.

7. Aeschynanthus humilis:
- Small plant with elongated leaves that grow to different sizes.
- Often associated with good fortune and used in decorative arrangements.

8. Aeschynanthus tricolor:
- Thick, ridged leaves that are dark green with contrasting veins.
- Prefers high humidity and protection from direct sunlight.

9. Aeschynanthus ‘Rasta’:
- Twisted, curly leaves with bright red flowers.
- Grows well in partial sunlight and requires high humidity.

10. Aeschynanthus pulcher:
- Heart-shaped leaves with a silvery shade, known for its precise growing needs.
- Requires high humidity and bright, indirect light.

11. Aeschynanthus evrardii:
- Metallic purplish-black foliage that deepens with age.
- Prefers high humidity and bright, indirect light.

12. Aeschynanthus hildebrandii:
- Deep burgundy leaves that appear almost black with a glossy surface.
- Grows well in typical Aeschynanthus conditions with indirect light and high humidity.
