Light

| Medium/High

Epiphyllum prefers bright, indirect light. Ideal conditions are filtered sunlight or dappled shade. Avoid direct sunlight, which can scorch the leaves. Indoor placement near an east or north-facing window for optimal growth.

Water

| Medium/High

Epiphyllum requires consistent moisture but does not tolerate soggy soil. Water when the top inch of soil feels dry. Water deeply, allowing excess water to drain away.

Soil

| Well-Drained

Use a well-draining, organic-rich potting mix. A blend of cactus soil mixed with orchid bark, peat moss, or perlite. Slightly acidic to neutral pH (pH 5.0 - 6.5).

Humidity

| Medium/High

-Temperatures of 60°F to 80°F (15°C to 27°C). Avoid cold temperatures below 50°F (10°C) can harm the plant. Prefers moderate to high humidity (50% - 70%). Mist occasionally if the air is too dry.


Fern, commonly referred to as Bracken or Maidenhair, is a captivating group of non-flowering vascular plants renowned for their lush, feathery fronds. The group includes around 10,560 species, each boasting unique leaf shapes, sizes, and growth habits. Ferns are prized for their ornamental value and are popular both as houseplants and garden specimens.


Fertilization

Feed Echeveria with a balanced, water-soluble fertilizer diluted to half strength once a month during the growing season (spring and summer). Avoid fertilizing in the fall and winter when the plant’s growth slows down.

Maintenance

Remove any dead or dried leaves from the base of the plant to maintain its appearance and prevent pests. If the plant becomes leggy, it can be trimmed, and the cuttings can be propagated.

Physical Characteristics

Epiphyllum features flat, segmented stems that are often mistaken for leaves. These stems can be smooth or wavy, and their pendulous growth habit makes them perfect for hanging baskets. Flowers are large, tubular, and can be white, pink, red, yellow, or even bi-colored.

Common Names

Orchid Cactus, Leaf Cactus, Climbing Cactus

Order: Caryophyllales | Family: Cactaceae | Genus: Epiphyllum | Species: Various (e.g., Epiphyllum oxypetalum, Epiphyllum anguliger)


Common Species

1. Epiphyllum oxypetalum (Queen of the Night):
- Known for its fragrant, white, night-blooming flowers.

2. Epiphyllum anguliger (Fishbone Cactus):
- Recognized for its wavy, zig-zag stems and striking white or yellow flowers.

3. Epiphyllum ‘Ackermannii’:
- Features bright red flowers and robust growth.
