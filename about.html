What is
The Leaf Lounge?

Taking care of your plants is hard enough without having to constantly search for care guides or remember watering schedules. The Leaf Lounge is designed to take the guesswork out of caring for your houseplants by putting clear, simple information all in one place.

In The Garden, you can browse a collection of many common houseplants organized by genus, making it easier to discover plants that are closely related and often share similar care needs.

At a glance, each plant card provides a quick preview along with basic information about watering and sunlight requirements so you can quickly understand what your plant needs. From there, you can choose to learn more about the plant and explore additional details about its care, or add it directly to your device’s calendar.

When you add a plant, The Leaf Lounge generates a recurring reminder tailored to that plant’s watering schedule, helping you stay on track with regular care.

By combining accessible plant information with simple scheduling tools, the goal of The Leaf Lounge is to make plant care feel easier, more organized, and a little more relaxing.
